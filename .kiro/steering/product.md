# Product

Luci Studio is a personal portfolio and blog website for Trung Lập (Luci), a Software Engineer and Flutter Developer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

## Purpose
- Showcase professional experience, projects, and skills
- Display creative/generative art work
- Host a technical blog (currently linking to Medium)
- Serve as a contact point for freelance and employment opportunities

## Owner
- **Name:** Trung Lập (Luci)
- **Specialization:** Flutter development, cross-platform mobile apps, clean architecture
- **Notable work:** memoirMe (E2E encrypted diary), TBchat (blockchain messaging), QuanhTa (real estate marketplace)

## Sections
- **Hero** – Animated landing with role badge, headline, CTA buttons, and stat pills
- **About** – Personal story, quick facts grid
- **Work / Portfolio** – Project cards (memoirMe, TBchat, QuanhTa, Survive in Shadow)
- **Blog** – Article previews (links to Medium)
- **Experience** – Expandable timeline of professional and indie projects
- **Art Gallery** – Generative/creative art grid
- **Contact** – Call-to-action section

## Deployment
Deployed on **Cloudflare Pages** via Wrangler, serving static assets from the `./dist` directory with edge delivery.
